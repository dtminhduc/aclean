AClean – Photo & Video Cleaner

Free up space on your iPhone and iPad. Everything is processed on your device.

Is anything deleted automatically?

No. Everything you clean goes to the AClean Bin first. Photos are only deleted when you empty the Bin and confirm in the iOS dialog, and they stay in “Recently Deleted” in Photos for 30 days.

Why is the space estimate “upper”?

If “Optimize iPhone Storage” is on, many originals live in iCloud, so cleaning frees iCloud space more than device space. Items only in iCloud are skipped unless you allow downloading.

What does Pro unlock?

A one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks bulk actions: keep the best shot in every group, clean every duplicate at once, shrink the whole library, compress several videos in a row and fix many phone numbers at once. Scanning and cleaning items one by one are free.

I bought Pro but it’s locked

Open Settings › AClean Pro › Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Contacts changed by mistake?

AClean saves a backup before every change. Open the Contacts tool › Backup › Export latest backup, then open the .vcf file with the Contacts app.
